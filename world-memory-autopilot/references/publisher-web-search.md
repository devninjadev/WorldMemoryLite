# Search results as a news feed

This default news mode replaces RSS acquisition and article-access admission gates. Keep market validation, Notion schemas, Report formatting and confirmed-write ordering unchanged.

4. Use publisher-web-search-v2 as a news candidate feed. Follow world-memory-autopilot references/publisher-web-search.md. Search Bloomberg/bloomberg.com, FT/ft.com, WSJ/wsj.com, Barron's/barrons.com, Benzinga/benzinga.com and MarketWatch/marketwatch.com individually. Do not call collect-feeds/read-feed-page or retrieve RSS.app. Use a day-level lookback covering the resolved window plus 24-hour indexing overlap, one broad and one complementary focused query per publisher, and at most one gap follow-up per publisher. Validate actual domains; exclude off-domain discovery hits, ads, quote pages and generic landing pages. Record search and article access separately. Article access unavailable is an access state, not a news collection error. For a publisher already known to deny full-text access in the baseline, skip routine article opens and use source-attributed search excerpts plus independent follow-up searches; do not repeatedly probe the same denial. A corroborated claim counts as successful evidence collection and does not incur a data gap or confidence/data-quality downgrade solely because full text is unavailable. Preserve access=unavailable and verification=corroborated separately in provenance. Actual failed searches, unsupported material details, contradictions, and missing coverage remain errors or gaps; syndication never creates independent corroboration. Original-article access is optional enrichment, not an admission prerequisite. Respect denials; never retrieve blocked articles via alternate surfaces or reconstruct them from snippets.
Read the exact previous Report and related Collection for canonical-URL and semantic event comparison; missing required baseline means novelty-baseline-unavailable and no writes. No prior Report means initial baseline. Search titles and substantive source-attributed snippets are feed evidence: preserve their URL, publisher, retrieval time, displayed date and literal supported claims. A single clear snippet may support an explicitly attributed reported claim (search-summary-only), not independent confirmation. Bare titles or ambiguous/truncated snippets are leads only. Do not infer missing details or copy an engine-generated multi-source answer as publisher text.
For each material event selected for a key takeaway or Story, perform targeted follow-up searches for its event, entities and decision-relevant numbers. Use other named publishers and official releases/filings/regulators/central banks first; reputable independent reporting may supplement, separately labeled. Budget up to two follow-up searches per selected event, prioritize up to six material events per run. Cross-check claims individually; retain corroborated facts, attribute single-source reports, omit unsupported numbers/details and mark contradictions unresolved. Independently authored, source-attributed search excerpts can corroborate a claim without full-page access; repeated results, syndication and copies of one source are not independent. No forced second confirmation of an exclusive: describe it as that publisher's report with appropriate uncertainty. Material uncorroborated allegations or disputed claims belong in watch-items, not factual key takeaways or confirmed Story state.
5. Separate freshness from fact confidence. Use exact publication/update timestamps when supplied; otherwise preserve date-only or relative date with retrievedAt and timing uncertainty. Never invent timezones, times or assign retrieval/crawl time as publication time. Exact timestamps in [Window Start,Window End) mean in-window; a date/relative range plausibly overlapping the window is coarse-window; unknown publication time is newly-discovered only, never newly-published. Semantic novelty against the persisted baseline is mandatory in all cases. Known old items outside the overlap are context only; within the 24-hour overlap label late-discovered. Title/timestamp refresh alone is duplicate. Unknown-time items with clear current-event evidence may enter with an explicit timing caveat; old or indeterminate relevance stays a lead/gap. Do not reject an otherwise supported new report solely for missing full text or minute-level time.
Continue to market and Report/Story workflow when at least one substantive novel search-summary-only or corroborated event supports a useful limited Report. Confidence and gaps reflect evidence depth; do not fill narrative with invented details. Lead-only candidates cannot alone justify a Report. If none qualifies, write nothing; use ::SKIP_COMPLETION:: only after adequate coverage finds no change, otherwise explain coverage failure.
Create Collection with existing schema, omit RSS Feed Success Count and Feed Failure Count, and do not use RSS builders. Store concise evidence records in prose: itemId, publisher/URL/title, supported claims, evidence access, verification status, corroborating URLs and each claim's support, displayed date/time precision and basis, retrievedAt, freshness class, novelty and baseline comparison. Record all six publishers' query/candidate/retained/duplicate/unresolved counts and search/access errors. Use existing properties only. Preserve this evidence index in Report sources-and-data if Collection fails. Cite the sources actually seen, label search-summary-only claims in Korean, disclose sampled coverage and indexing delay. Carry only supported claim summaries and explicit uncertainty into the validated plan; keep uncorroborated Story content as attributed reporting, never silently upgrade it to confirmed fact. Do not persist raw tool responses or temporary plans.

## Evidence examples

- A blocked Bloomberg article has a substantive snippet saying a company is considering an IPO: retain that attributed consideration, not a completed IPO or absent valuation. Seek filings and other reporting, but lack of corroboration does not erase the report.
- Two independently authored excerpts agree on an announced amount but have date-only metadata: corroborate the amount while retaining date precision; never invent midnight.
- Three sites repeat one FT scoop: one reporting origin, not three confirmations.
- An old article crawled today is not new. An undated discovery needs current-event support and semantic novelty or stays a lead.
- Conflicting amounts must be reconciled for period/currency before use; otherwise omit the number or put the dispute in watch-items.

Evidence statuses in prose are corroborated, search-summary-only, lead-only and disputed. They do not add fields or enum values to the closed Report/Story plan or Notion schema. Map overall confidence and data quality to existing allowed values.
